menu_book HOW BORROWING WORKS

Borrowing from the Owner's Shelf

STEP 01 Read In-House

Feel free to grab any book from the shelves and read while having your coffee. Just return it before leaving.

STEP 02 Borrow or Buy

Found a favorite? Borrow it for up to 14 days with a reader pass, or buy a brand-new copy to take home.

STEP 03 Share & Recommend

Leave a sticky note review inside the front cover or suggest books you'd love to see on the shelves next.

READER PASS #MEM-042

Monthly Reader Pass

Borrow up to 3 books at a time to take home, plus enjoy 10% off all coffee and pastries at the counter.
