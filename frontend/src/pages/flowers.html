CARE & ETHICAL SOURCING

Studio Stewardship & Sourcing Principles

Daily Morning Delivery

We partner directly with family flower farms in New York and Connecticut. Blooms are cut at dawn and reach our ceramic basins by mid-morning.

Chemical-Free Preservation

Our dried stems undergo zero chemical bleaching or petroleum dyeing. Everything is hang-dried in our well-ventilated loft rafters.

Pick-up & Packaging

Orders can be claimed at the floral studio counter during all operating hours. We wrap in unbleached paper with an enclosed stem conditioning guide.
