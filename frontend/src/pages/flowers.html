CARE & ETHICAL SOURCING

Studio Stewardship & Sourcing Principles

Daily Morning Delivery

We partner directly with local organic flower farms. Blooms are cut at dawn and reach our ceramic basins by mid-morning.

Chemical-Free Preservation

Our dried stems and artisanal crafts undergo zero chemical bleaching or petroleum dyeing. Everything is dried and assembled with care.

Pick-up & Packaging

Orders can be claimed at the floral studio counter during all operating hours. We wrap in unbleached paper with an enclosed stem conditioning guide.
